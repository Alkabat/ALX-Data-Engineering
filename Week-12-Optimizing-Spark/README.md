One of the important lessons from this section was that slow Spark applications can have different underlying causes, including inefficient transformations, excessive shuffling, unsuitable partitioning, data skew, and resource constraints.

I also reinforced the importance of reading complete error messages and investigating the context of a failure rather than relying on assumptions.

The practical exercises helped connect these concepts to the way Spark applications are developed, executed, monitored, and debugged.
