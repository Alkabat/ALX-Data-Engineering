## Reflections

This week helped me understand that building a data pipeline involves more than simply moving data from one location to another. A reliable pipeline must be properly structured, automated, scalable, and observable.

Setting up my first Airflow environment and creating a DAG gave me practical experience with workflow orchestration. I gained a better understanding of how tasks can be arranged to run in the correct order and how schedules and triggers can be used to automate pipeline execution.

I also learned that scalability should be considered during the design stage of a data system. Factors such as concurrency, parallelism, caching, efficient coding, data-source characteristics, downstream processing, and cloud-provider selection can significantly affect system performance.

The e-commerce exercise showed me how poor scalability can affect business operations. Problems such as database contention, delayed inventory updates, and slow reporting can lead to financial losses and poor customer experiences.

Overall, this week strengthened my understanding of scalable data engineering. I now have a clearer view of how tools such as Apache Airflow, Docker, and Hadoop contribute to the development of efficient, reliable, and automated data-processing systems.
